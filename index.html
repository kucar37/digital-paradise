THE ARTIST

From Eldrick Alvarado to Digital Paradise.

Digital Paradise is a DJ and music producer driven by an endless love for music and a deep desire to make people dance. Based in the sun-soaked paradise of Aruba, this versatile artist is known for vibrant Caribbean party vibes while also exploring a wide range of genres — from smooth reggae to high-energy tech house.

Inspired by the forward-thinking vision of Jeon, the lasting influence of DJ Chuckie, and local icon Bradley Fonseca, Digital Paradise embraces a relaxed and open-minded approach to music production.

Through a unique blend of music selection, refined mixing techniques, and a powerful stage presence, Digital Paradise excels at creating unforgettable experiences.
